---
name: Case-study evidence boundaries
description: Preserve the distinction between product intent and observed prototype history in the TV case study.
---

Present available-renderer captures as evidence of current renderable versions, not as archival screenshots. Separate requested design directions from observed implementation, and distinguish brief feedback references from stored annotation identifiers.

**Why:** The case-study brief and live prototype disagree in several places, and seeded version dates do not establish a reliable historical schedule. The user explicitly requires a real account without invented research, outcomes, dates, screenshots or attribution.

**How to apply:** Treat the case study as an evidence-led explanation, not authorization to modify the TV prototype to fit the story. Re-check both implementation and stored feedback when updating claims. Document missing evidence rather than interpreting it as proof that an activity never happened.
