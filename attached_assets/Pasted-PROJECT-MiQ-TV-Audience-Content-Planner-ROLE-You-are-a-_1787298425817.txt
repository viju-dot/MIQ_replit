PROJECT: MiQ TV Audience & Content Planner

ROLE
You are a senior Product Designer, UX Researcher, Data Visualization Designer,
and Product Analyst working on a MiQ TV Audience & Content Planner.

Your task is to create a functional requirements document and the supporting
research/design system documentation needed to ideate and build an AI-powered
functional prototype in Replit.

PROJECT CONTEXT

Goal:
Build a TV audience and content planner dashboard that provides content and
viewership consumption insights based on demographic audience inputs.

Markets:
- UK
- Four additional European markets

Primary users:
- Sales teams
- Trader teams

V1:
- Single-page analytical dashboard
- Approximately 8 core KPI/insight areas
- Insights should be connected through filtering and drilldown rather than
  behaving as independent charts.

PRIMARY PRODUCT QUESTIONS

The dashboard should help users understand:

1. What does my audience watch?
2. Where do they watch it?
3. When do they watch it?
4. Which genres are most relevant?
5. Which shows over-index for this audience?
6. Which platforms contribute to viewing?
7. What other genres does this audience have an affinity for?
8. Where does the audience go after watching content?
9. Where are the commercial opportunities for sellers?
10. How does one audience compare with another?

CORE KPI / INSIGHT REQUIREMENTS

1. CONSUMPTION BY TYPE

User question:
"How much time does my audience spend watching streaming, FAST, linear
and homescreen, and which one should I activate on to reach them?"

Metrics:
- % of minutes watched per household

Dimensions:
- Linear
- Streaming
- FAST
- Homescreen

Requirements:
- Interactive visual
- Selected type should act as a secondary filter for downstream insights
- Global viewing environment filter:
  - All
  - Commercial only
- Commercial only should represent commercially addressable opportunities
  and exclude non-commercial environments where relevant, such as BBC.

Recommended interaction:
- All
- Linear
- Streaming
- FAST
- Homescreen

The selected consumption type should dynamically update relevant visualisations
below.

2. TIME SPENT BY CHANNEL / APP

User question:
"How much time does my audience spend watching specific apps or channels?"

Metrics:
- Minutes watched per week
- % of total watch time
- Index of viewership vs national average

Dimensions:
- Linear
- Streaming
- FAST

Requirements:
- Support top 10 apps/channels
- Streaming examples:
  Netflix, Amazon Prime, Disney+, Paramount+, Apple TV, YouTube
- Linear examples:
  BBC iPlayer, ITVX, Channel 4, NOW, My5, U
- Support platform/type filtering
- Commercial-only logic should be available
- Explore app overlap as a secondary interaction, not a mandatory core chart.

Recommended visual:
- Bar + line combination chart
- Bars = average minutes watched per week
- Line = % of total watch time
- Index displayed as a supporting value/indicator

3. TEMPORAL INSIGHTS

User question:
"When does my audience most watch TV, and what type of TV do they watch
and when?"

Metrics:
- Average minutes watched per household
- % of households watching

Dimensions:
- Linear
- Streaming
- FAST
- Day of week
- Time of day
- App/channel depending on selected consumption type

Requirements:
- Allow selection of Linear, Streaming or FAST
- Allow channel/app filtering based on selected type
- Show behaviour across the week
- Avoid changing the original metric definition into session-based metrics
  unless explicitly requested.

Recommended visual:
- Heatmap
- Day of week × time of day

4. VIEWERSHIP BY GENRE

User question:
"What genre is most popular with my audience and on what TV type do they
most consume this genre?"

Metrics:
- Minutes watched per week
- % of total watch time
- Index vs national average

Dimensions:
- Genre
- Linear
- Streaming
- FAST

Genres should use the IAB taxonomy.

Example genres:
- Comedy
- News
- Film
- Sports
- Drama
- Reality
- Action
- Documentary
- Kids
- Travel
- Food
- Home improvement

Recommended visual:
- Horizontal bar chart
- Show minutes prominently
- Show % as supporting information
- Show directional index at the end of each bar

IMPORTANT:
Viewership by Genre and Cultural Affinity are NOT duplicates.

Viewership by Genre answers:
"What does my audience watch most?"

Cultural Affinity answers:
"What genres does my audience unusually over-index on compared with
the national average?"

5. TOP 10 INDEXING SHOWS

User question:
"Which shows are particularly relevant to my audience?"

Metrics:
- Average minutes watched per household
- % of households watching
- Index vs national average

Dimensions:
- Show

Requirements:
- View across all available data feeds
- Top indexing shows
- DoY for 7 and 15 day lookbacks
- WoW for 30 and 60 day lookbacks
- Dropdown can expose top 50 shows
- Searchable list
- If a genre is selected in Viewership by Genre, filter the shows accordingly

IMPORTANT:
Do not reinterpret this as a generic "Top Trending Shows" chart.
The primary purpose is indexing against the national average.

6. SHARE OF VIEWING BY PLATFORM

User question:
"For a selected genre, where does my audience consume it?"

Metrics:
- Share of viewing minutes

Dimensions:
- Genre
- Linear
- Streaming
- FAST

Requirements:
- Genre-level comparison
- Do not make this a channel-level chart
- Selected genre should be connected to Viewership by Genre

Recommended visual:
- 100% horizontal stacked bar
- Each bar = one genre
- Segments = Linear / Streaming / FAST

Example:
Drama:
Linear 50% | Streaming 40% | FAST 10%

7. CULTURAL AFFINITY

User question:
"What other genres does my audience have a strong affinity for?"

Metric:
- Index vs national average

Dimension:
- Genre

Requirements:
- Selected genre is the anchor
- Show at least top 5 other high-index genres
- Clicking an indexed genre should reveal its top indexing shows
- Use index as the primary analytical signal

Recommended visual:
- Star diagram as requested by product
- Also evaluate readability against a bar-based alternative during
  data-visualisation research.

IMPORTANT RELATIONSHIP:
Viewership by Genre
→ Select genre
→ Cultural Affinity
→ Select highly indexed genre
→ Top Indexing Shows

8. MIGRATION SIGNALS

User question:
"After watching a show, where does my audience go next?"

Metric:
- Audience transition event/pathway

Dimension:
- Show

Requirements:
- Timeframe of 1 hour
- Show next show watched
- Show associated network/platform
- Top 3 pathways at each branch
- Maximum 3 branches
- Avoid visual spaghetti

Recommended visual:
- Sankey chart

Example:
Selected Show
→ Netflix / Show B
→ ITVX / Show C
→ Channel 4 / Show D

GLOBAL CAPABILITIES

A. COMMERCIAL ONLY

This is a global viewing-environment filter.

All:
- Include available viewing environments

Commercial only:
- Exclude relevant non-commercial environments
- Focus the analysis on environments representing potential seller
  opportunities

Do not treat Commercial Only as merely another content category.

B. COMPARISON MODE

This is a global capability, not a separate KPI.

Purpose:
Allow two audiences to be compared across the dashboard.

Example:
Audience A:
Female, 25–34

Audience B:
Male, 25–34

The same KPI visualisations should update to show differences between the
two audiences.

Comparison Mode is currently considered a P2 capability, but the V1 design
should be structurally ready for it.

MARKET-SPECIFIC FILTER LOGIC

UK:
- Mosaic groups
- Age
- Gender
- More granular demographic inputs

Other four European markets:
- Simpler TV consumption inputs
- Heavy
- Medium
- Light

Do not assume that UK filter logic can be copied directly to all markets.

DATA / PERFORMANCE CONSTRAINTS

Backend strategy:
- Multiple RDS tables
- Multiple APIs
- Do not assume one massive table

Design implications:
- Avoid unnecessary simultaneous API calls
- Use progressive loading where appropriate
- Design charts so that partial data/loading states can be represented
- Consider performance when proposing a single-page dashboard with
  approximately 8 core visualisations.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES

1. Do not create charts just to fill the dashboard.
2. Prefer connected analytical journeys.
3. Every visualisation must answer a clear user question.
4. Use drilldown and filtering to reduce dashboard density.
5. Avoid duplicate insights.
6. Prioritise commercial/actionable insights.
7. Make index vs national average easy to understand.
8. Maintain consistent interaction patterns.
9. Support responsive loading/skeleton states.
10. Design for Sales and Trader workflows rather than generic analytics users.

ANALYTICAL JOURNEY

Structure the experience around:

UNDERSTAND
→ Consumption by Type
→ Time Spent by Channel/App
→ Temporal Insights

DISCOVER
→ Viewership by Genre
→ Share of Viewing by Platform
→ Cultural Affinity
→ Top Indexing Shows
→ Migration Signals

COMPARE
→ Comparison Mode

ACTIVATE / OPPORTUNITY
→ Commercial Only
→ Future recommendation opportunities

IMPORTANT PRODUCT PRINCIPLE

The dashboard should move beyond:
"What does this audience watch?"

towards:
"Where can I commercially reach this audience, what content is relevant,
and what should I consider activating?"

AI IDEATION REQUIREMENTS

Use AI prototype ideation to explore:
- Cross-filtering
- Drilldowns
- Connected KPI journeys
- Audience comparison
- Commercial opportunity discovery
- Content recommendations
- Platform recommendations
- Future incremental-reach opportunities

Do NOT add new KPIs simply because they look visually interesting.

Potential future ideas such as:
- Incremental reach opportunity
- Audience overlap
- Reach vs frequency opportunity
- Competitive viewership
- AI activation recommendations

should be treated as FUTURE OPPORTUNITIES unless they are explicitly
confirmed as V1 requirements.

COMPETITION RESEARCH

Competition research should focus specifically on MiQ's competitive
landscape and positioning.

Investigate relevant capabilities from:
- The Trade Desk
- Google Display & Video 360
- Nielsen ONE
- Samba TV
- Comscore

Do not copy competitors blindly.

For each competitor capability:
- Identify the user problem it solves
- Determine whether MiQ already has an equivalent capability
- Identify a gap
- Determine whether the gap is relevant to Sales/Trader users
- Recommend whether it belongs in V1, P2 or Future

The objective is to identify opportunities that strengthen MiQ's
differentiation around:
- TV audience intelligence
- Cross-platform viewing
- Reach
- Frequency
- Content intelligence
- Commercial opportunity
- Planning
- Activation
