Task: Create the TV Audience Content Planner — Phase 1 Scope & Functional Requirements Document from V5
Objective

Create a complete, production-ready Functional Requirements & Scope Document for the TV Audience Content Planner — Phase 1, using the V5 AI prototype as the primary source of truth.

The purpose of this document is to translate the V5 prototype into a clear specification that can be used by Product, UX/UI, Data Visualisation, Engineering and Data teams to understand exactly what needs to be built.

Do not redesign the product. Do not invent new KPIs, features, data, interactions or requirements.

Document what exists in V5, including the latest stakeholder feedback and the final agreed behaviour represented in the prototype.

Use the following priority order:

V5 prototype — primary source of truth
