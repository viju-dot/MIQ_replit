==================================================
1. PROJECT CONTEXT
==================================================

This is a TV audience and content planner dashboard for MiQ.

Primary users:
- Sales teams
- Trader teams

Primary goal:
Help users understand how a selected audience consumes TV content across
Linear TV, Streaming and FAST environments and identify useful content,
platform and audience insights.

The dashboard should help answer:

- What does my audience watch?
- Where do they watch?
- When do they watch?
- Which shows/genres are important?
- Which genres over-index for this audience?
- Where does the audience move after content ends?

The dashboard is designed as a single-page analytical experience.

20. INDEX VS NATIONAL AVERAGE
==================================================

Index must be consistently explained.

Baseline:
100 = national average

Above 100:
Audience over-indexes

Below 100:
Audience under-indexes

Example:

Index 150
= audience is 50% higher than national average

Index 80
= audience is 20% lower than national average

Do not make the user calculate this manually.

Use directional indicators where appropriate.
